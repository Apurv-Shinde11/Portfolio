# Earth imagery and attribution

Credit: NASA Earth Observatory.

These are historical surface/light composites, not live weather or live city-light measurements.
Only solar illumination is calculated from the current UTC time.

- day.webp: Blue Marble Next Generation January 2004 base map, NASA Earth Observatory.
  Source: https://assets.science.nasa.gov/content/dam/science/esd/eo/images/bmng/bmng-base/january/world.200401.3x5400x2700.jpg

- night.webp: Black Marble 2016 global color composite, NASA Earth Observatory / NASA Earth Observatory images by Joshua Stevens, using Suomi NPP VIIRS data from Miguel Román, NASA GSFC.
  Source: https://assets.science.nasa.gov/content/dam/science/esd/eo/images/imagerecords/144000/144898/BlackMarble_2016_01deg.jpg
  Catalog: https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/earth-at-night/maps/

NASA media usage: https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/
NASA imagery is generally usable for informational purposes with acknowledgement,
without implying NASA endorsement. These maps are NASA-attributed imagery and have
no separately indicated third-party copyright restriction in their catalog entries.

Both maps cover longitude -180 to +180 and latitude +90 to -90, in a 2:1
equirectangular projection. Resampled to 2048 x 1024, WebP quality 90, no geographic
crop, offset, mirroring or color manipulation.
